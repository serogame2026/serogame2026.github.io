Your city is on the belt.

Pick any of 173 countries and sort boxes for your own cities. A player in São Paulo sees “São Paulo” on a box — and keeps playing.

  • Simple to start, hard to put down
  • The belt starts calm and speeds up as you deliver
  • Short runs — perfect for a quick break
  • Leave any time; saved shifts continue exactly where you stopped

Modes

More ways to play.

12 MISSIONS

Supervisor missions

Two missions close every world. Each one tests a different reflex — from “Zero Mistakes” to “The Belt Lost Its Mind”.

216 CHALLENGES

Challenge

Any country, any difficulty. Against Time or Against Lives, 1 to 8 trucks, 10 parts each. Medals light up as you win.

GLOBAL BOARDS

World Record

A 200-second sprint or a 5-lives marathon. Weekly boards reset — a fresh race to #1 every week.

PRACTICE

Training

Replay any of the 120 levels or 12 missions. Your real career is never touched.

SERO ECONOMY

Earn & spend SERO

Daily shift stamps, work orders and a weekly goal. Spend SERO at the canteen on perks like +1 life or +10 seconds.

DRONE BREAK

Slack off

Take a short drone break mid-shift and fly around the world you work in.

Status

Not a game about work.
A job you play.

Clock in, work your shift, get promoted. Every player who reaches Manager joins the public Company Managers board — numbered, dated, forever.

Rookie→Apprentice→Line Chief→Shift Supervisor→Manager→Regional Manager→Logistics Director→The CEO’s chair
  • 235 badges and 419 medals to collect
  • Ranks and titles other players can see
  • “What kind of staff?” — a 7-day HR report built from how you actually play
